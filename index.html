👤 About This Project

Matthew's Paleo HQ is a personal avocational paleontology portfolio: part field notebook, part learning log, part fossil catalog, part dinosaur encyclopedia, and part unabashed celebration of prehistoric life.

The goal is simple: take a lifelong fascination with dinosaurs, fossils, evolution, and deep time and turn it into something tangible—courses completed, books read, field trips taken, fossils documented, scientific concepts learned, and original educational work created.

This site intentionally has the personality of a late-1990s / early-2000s dinosaur website: beveled buttons, dark reds, sunset amber, chunky panels, and the faint sense that somewhere there should be a MIDI file playing.

Scientific standard: enthusiasm is encouraged; bullshitting is not. Research notes and future dinosaur dossiers should distinguish direct evidence from inference and speculation.

This site is an independent personal project and is not affiliated with any film studio, dinosaur franchise, museum, university, fossil organization, or government agency.
