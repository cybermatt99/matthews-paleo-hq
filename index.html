ABOUT

The person behind the terminal.

JURASSIC MATT

I work in cybersecurity in Florida and have loved dinosaurs for as long as I can remember. In 2026 I decided to take that interest more seriously through structured study, field experience, fossil collecting, research notes, and this public archive.

This site is not meant to present me as a professional paleontologist. It documents an amateur learning seriously, building skills, joining the fossil community, and keeping track of the things that make prehistoric life fascinating.

Cybersecurity footnote: Nedry probably would have had a harder time if I were on the park's security team.
